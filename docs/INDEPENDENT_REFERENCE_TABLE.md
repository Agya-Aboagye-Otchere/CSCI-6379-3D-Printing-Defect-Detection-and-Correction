## 1. Methodological Independence Notice

In accordance with the project proposal and team work plan, Research Question 3 evaluates whether the rule table can reliably match expert manufacturing recommendations. To avoid self-referential bias (where the author of the rule table also provides the answers), this reference benchmark was authored independently using established international additive manufacturing standards and published troubleshooting guides:
- **Prusa Research**: *3D Printing Handbook & Knowledge Base* (2023)
- **Simplify3D**: *Comprehensive Print Quality Troubleshooting Guide* (2022)

- **Gibson, Rosen, Stucker**: *Additive Manufacturing Technologies* (Springer, 2021)

## 2. Independent Reference Recommendation Table

| Defect Category | Root Physical Cause | Primary Corrective Action | Secondary Action | Hardware / Mechanical Check | Source References |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stringing / Oozing** | Residual nozzle pressure and molten polymer fluidity during non-print travel moves, allowing low-viscosity filament to weep out of the nozzle orifice. | **retraction_distance** (INCREASE): `+1.0 to +2.0 mm (Bowden) or +0.5 to +1.0 mm (Direct Drive)` | **nozzle_temperature** (DECREASE): `-5 to -10 °C` | Inspect nozzle tip for partial erosion; ensure filament is dried (hygroscopic moisture boils and forces oozing). | Simplify3D Print Quality Guide: Stringing and Oozing (2022)<br>Prusa Knowledge Base: Stringing and Oozing Solutions (2023) |
| **Warping / Thermal Shrinkage** | Differential thermal contraction and internal cooling stress gradients between newly deposited hot upper layers and cooling lower layers in contact with the print bed. | **bed_temperature** (INCREASE): `+5 to +10 °C (below polymer glass transition Tg)` | **cooling_fan_speed** (DECREASE): `-20% to -50% (turn off completely for first layers)` | Clean bed surface with Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA); apply adhesive brim/raft; eliminate ambient room drafts/enclosure draft. | Gibson, Rosen, & Stucker, Additive Manufacturing Technologies (Springer, 2021)<br>Prusa Research: Warping Prevention in Extrusion Printing (2023) |
| **Layer Shifting / Step Misalignment** | Stepper motor torque failure, belt tooth skipping, mechanical binding along X/Y axes, or nozzle collision with curled overhangs causing skipped stepper micro-steps. | **travel_and_print_speed** (DECREASE): `-15% to -25% (reduce acceleration and jerk)` | **stepper_motor_current_or_jerk** (DECREASE_JERK): `Lower maximum jerk from 10 to 7 mm/s` | Tighten loose timing belts (X/Y axis); check set screws on pulleys; lubricate linear rods; inspect stepper driver overheating. | All3DP: 3D Printer Layer Shift – How to Fix It (2023)<br>Simplify3D Troubleshooting: Layer Shifting and Misalignment (2022) |
| **Under-Extrusion** | Volumetric material delivery rate is less than the commanded toolpath volume, resulting in inter-bead voids, weak perimeters, and porous infill. | **flow_rate_extrusion_multiplier** (INCREASE): `+5% to +10%` | **nozzle_temperature** (INCREASE): `+5 to +10 °C (lowers melt viscosity and backpressure)` | Check for partial nozzle clog (perform cold pull); verify extruder drive gear tension; ensure filament diameter matches slicer setting. | Simplify3D Print Quality Guide: Under-Extrusion (2022)<br>Prusa 3D Printing Handbook: Extrusion and Flow Troubleshooting (2023) |
| **Over-Extrusion** | Excessive volumetric deposition exceeding the sliced bead envelope, causing plastic bulging, dimensional inaccuracy, nozzle dragging, and surface blobbing. | **flow_rate_extrusion_multiplier** (DECREASE): `-5% to -10%` | **nozzle_temperature** (DECREASE): `-5 °C (curbs excessive thermal expansion and runniness)` | Calibrate extruder E-steps/mm; measure actual filament diameter with digital calipers (e.g. 1.78mm vs 1.75mm). | Prusa Knowledge Base: Over-Extrusion Calibration (2023)<br>All3DP: Over-Extrusion in 3D Printing – Causes and Solutions (2023) |
| **Bed Adhesion Failure** | Insufficient intermolecular bonding between the first thermoplastic layer and the build plate, caused by improper nozzle-to-bed gap (Z-offset), contamination, or non-uniform leveling. | **z_offset_nozzle_gap** (DECREASE): `-0.05 to -0.10 mm (lower nozzle closer to bed for proper squish)` | **bed_temperature** (INCREASE): `+5 to +10 °C` | Re-tram/level build plate corners; degrease PEI/glass sheet with 90%+ IPA or warm dish soap; apply adhesion promoter (glue stick/PVP). | Simplify3D Quality Guide: Not Sticking to the Bed (2022)<br>Prusa 3D Printing Handbook: First Layer Calibration (2023) |
